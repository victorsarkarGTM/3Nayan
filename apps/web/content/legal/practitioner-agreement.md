# Practitioner Agreement

This draft agreement sets out how practitioners offer sessions on 3Nayan.

## Your commitments

- You will not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe medication, or promise guaranteed outcomes.
- You will treat seekers with respect and keep what they share confidential.
- You will keep all communication and payments for 3Nayan sessions on 3Nayan.

## Fees and payouts

3Nayan charges a commission on each booking. Your share is paid to your verified bank account after each session, less applicable taxes.
