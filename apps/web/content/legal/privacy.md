# Privacy Policy

This draft explains what personal data 3Nayan collects and why, in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

## What we collect

- Your mobile number and name, to run your account.
- For practitioners: identity and bank verification results, credentials and an introduction video, to review applications and pay you.

## What we never store

We never store your full Aadhaar number or Aadhaar document. We keep only a verification result and the last four digits.

## Your rights

You can ask us to export or delete your data from Settings. Some records must be kept for legal and tax reasons.
