# Terms of Use

These draft terms describe how you may use 3Nayan, a marketplace connecting people with independent healers, astrologers and psychics.

## Not professional advice

Sessions on 3Nayan offer spiritual and personal guidance. They are not medical, legal or financial advice, and do not replace a qualified professional.

## Your account

You sign in with your mobile number. Keep your device secure and tell us if you suspect someone else has used your account.

## Practitioners

Practitioners are independent. 3Nayan verifies their identity and reviews their application, but does not guarantee any outcome of a session.
